# House style - The World Today

The standing rules behind every edition, stated once here so the
edition documents do not have to repeat them. The 24 November
2026 edition follows every rule below; where it bends one, the
bend is disclosed in that edition's own record.

## Selection

1. A story needs two distinct outlets carrying it on the day.
   One outlet, however good, is a rumour with a logo.
2. Brands of one owner count separately only when the feeds list
   them separately, and never as a story's sole two confirmations
   without disclosure. (This edition discloses the BBC pair.)
3. Features are chosen for consequence: a decision, an open
   toll, a reversal. Grid stories are chosen for weight: a fact
   the day cannot be understood without.
4. One argument piece and one assessment may be carried when two
   outlets stand behind them; they are labelled as what they are
   in the record.
5. No story is selected to balance another. Balance is an
   outcome the record may note, never a selection criterion.

## Headlines

6. Headlines are the carriers' wording in substance, shortened
   only to fit the column at the minimum font size.
7. Consequence before cause in features; attribution inside the
   headline when the story is someone's words.
8. No adjective the carriers did not use. No number the carriers
   did not print. No rounding, ever.
9. A headline that cannot fit is shortened editorially and the
   shortening is recorded in the story's dossier.

## Numbers

10. The strip holds at most one figure from the news, attributed
    in its label, plus counts of the edition itself, labelled as
    counts.
11. A figure is quotable when two outlets print it or when one
    named body's report is the story; in the second case the
    attribution travels with the figure everywhere it appears.
12. Tolls print as confirmed and missing, separately, as they
    stand at press time.

## Pictures

13. The lead image of a carrying article is the default
    thumbnail. Centre-crop only; no letterbox, no stretch, no
    filter.
14. A story without a usable photograph gets a panel, and the
    panel is a design element, not an apology.
15. No text is ever set on a photograph.

## Places and names

16. Places are named at the precision the record supports.
    Guessing a district the wires did not name is fabrication
    with good intentions.
17. People are named when the carriers name them in the
    headline's own story, not for colour.
18. Organisations' phrases stay in quotation marks with the
    organisation attached.

## Corrections

19. A correction belongs to the next edition's record, named
    against the edition it corrects. This repository's history
    is never rewritten to hide one.
20. The image of a submitted edition is never re-rendered except
    to prove determinism, which by construction changes nothing.

## The page

21. No body text. The format is masthead, date, headlines,
    thumbnails, numbers, sources. Depth lives in the linked
    reporting and in this repository's documents.
22. The date line is set at title scale. An edition misread as
    another day's has failed before it is read.
23. If a rule and the day's news conflict, the rule wins and the
    conflict is recorded. A style guide that bends silently is a
    press release.
