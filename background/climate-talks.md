# Why climate talks fail

Background for grid story 3 (the failure of COP31). The dossier
is stories/06-cop31.md.

## The three causes the analysis names

The BBC's analysis, which the page carries as an argument piece,
names three: the interests of oil-producing countries, a US
boycott, and a dispute over finance. The three are worth
separating because they fail differently:

- **Producer interests** are structural: a state whose revenue is
  hydrocarbons experiences a phase-out clause as a budget cut,
  and negotiates accordingly.
- **A boycott** is procedural: an absent major emitter changes
  what any agreement can claim to cover, and gives other parties
  a reason to withhold concessions.
- **Finance** is the standing dispute: who pays for adaptation
  and loss in the countries least responsible for emissions. It
  has closed more talks than any clause about targets.

## Why "failure" is the word

A round that ends without agreement is a failure in the narrow
sense the headline uses: the meeting's purpose was an agreement.
The analysis's larger claim, which the headline's question form
adopts, is that this failure is diagnostic: it shows a world
whose blocs no longer share a venue, a payer, or a premise. The
page prints that claim as its author's, labelled an analysis in
the record.

## Why the page carries an argument at all

A page of pure events cannot represent a day whose largest
long-term fact is a non-event: a negotiation that produced
nothing. The analysis piece is the format's answer, admitted
under the house rule that argument must be labelled and doubly
carried, and disclosed here and in OUTLETS.md as the edition's
weakest confirmation.

## What happens after a failed round

Failed rounds do not end processes; they move them. The finance
dispute typically reopens in smaller venues (fund boards,
bilateral deals, the next round's preparatory meetings) where
the cameras are fewer and the concessions cheaper. The dossier's
watch item is therefore not the next summit but the first venue
where the money question is tabled again. An edition that only
watches summits will report the failure and miss the sequel,
which is why the watch list in stories/06-cop31.md is written
the way it is.
