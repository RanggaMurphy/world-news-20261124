# Factory fires and the missing count

Background for feature 2 (the Indonesian factory fire). The
dossier is stories/02-factory-fire.md.

## Why "missing" is the operative word

In the first cycle of an industrial fire, the confirmed toll is
a floor, not a figure: it counts bodies recovered and identified.
The missing count is the ceiling's shadow: workers unaccounted
for after the shift roster is compared with the survivors. The
honest headline carries both numbers, which is why this edition's
does, in the carriers' own order: killed first, missing second.

## Why the toll moves

Three things move it, in the usual order: rescue reaching parts
of the building that were too hot or unstable to enter; hospitals
reporting deaths among the admitted; and the roster itself being
corrected, in both directions, as workers who fled are found
alive and workers on unofficial shifts are added. A toll that
only ever rises in the first day is normal and is not, by itself,
a sign of concealment.

## The industry context

Confectionery production concentrates the classic factory-fire
ingredients in one building: heat sources, packaging material in
bulk, fine organic dust, and shift work that fills the floor. The
page does not print a cause, because the carriers had not
established one; this background notes only why the sector's
fires are feared, which the word "feared" in the copy reflects.

## What the desk watches for next

- The missing count resolving, name by name or in an official
  consolidated figure.
- A stated cause from the fire service, which converts the story
  from a toll story into an accountability story.
- The owner's and the regulator's statements, which the first
  cycle rarely contains and the second cycle must.
