# Destroying degraded munitions

Background for feature 3 (the Iowa detonation ruling). The
dossier is stories/03-iowa-detonation.md.

## Why old explosives get detonated

Explosive fillers degrade. A stockpile that was safe to store
when manufactured can become chemically unstable with age, at
which point moving it is more dangerous than destroying it where
it sits. Open detonation at the holding plant is the standard
disposal method for material in that state, which is why the
Army's plan was a detonation and why the argument was never
about the method but about who authorises it.

## Why a court was involved at all

The sequence in the record is unusual: a planned disposal, a
presidential halt, a request that a court sign off on the safest
course, and a governor-elect written into the conditions. The
Eighth Circuit's clearance is therefore not a ruling on
explosives; it is a ruling that closes a public dispute about
authority. The headline prints the clearance because the
clearance is the act that lets everything else proceed.

## The governor-elect condition

Trump's statement made his own go-ahead subject to Rob Sand, the
incoming governor-elect, agreeing as well. A condition attached
to an office-holder who has not taken office is the detail that
lifted the story from the grid to the features: it is a fact
about how the decision was made, not just what was decided, and
process facts of that strangeness are what features are for.

## What the page did not need to decide

Whether detonation is in fact the safest course. The carriers
reported the dispute and the ruling; the engineering judgement
belongs to the Army and the court record, and a headline page
that pretended to hold it would be inventing expertise.

## The ammunition plant as a place

Ammunition plants are among the few industrial sites where a
community lives permanently beside a stockpile it did not choose
and cannot inspect. That standing fact explains the politics in
the record: a governor-elect's agreement is not a courtesy but a
recognition that the risk, however small the engineers judge it,
is borne locally while the decision is made federally. The
court's clearance settles the legal question; the condition in
Trump's statement acknowledges the political one. The page
prints the first and records the second, which is the correct
division for a format that can carry one fact per headline.
