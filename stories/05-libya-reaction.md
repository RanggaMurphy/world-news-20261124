# Story 5 - The Libya reaction (Around the World 2)

**Headline as printed:** Russian warship hit by Ukrainian drones
in Libya, Zelensky says

**Position:** Around the World, row 1, right cell, beside the
sinking it reacts to. Fact on the left, meaning on the right:
the grid's top row is the edition's clearest piece of structure.

**Carriers:** AP News, BBC News (two; a pair that shares no
outlet with the sinking's pair, part of why the two stories
could be printed separately).

**Region:** Libya / Russia.

## What happened

President Zelensky praised the drone operation that hit the
Russian warship in Libya, calling it exemplary. The Kremlin
warned of an escalation of tensions in the region and of a
dangerous trend after the Libya and Baltic attacks, but declined
to comment directly on the attacks themselves. Libya's UN-backed
government condemned the strike on its territory.

## The facts on record

- Zelensky praised the operation as exemplary.
- The Kremlin warned of an escalation of tensions in the region.
- Moscow declined to comment directly on the attacks.
- Russia also warned of a dangerous trend after Libya and
  Baltic attacks.

## Why it is printed separately

Merging this story into the sinking would have produced one
stronger story and a weaker page: on a page with no body text,
the only way to show both the event and its diplomatic shadow is
to print both. The duplication is the design, and it is
disclosed in SOURCES.md and EDITION.md so no future editor
"fixes" it by accident.

## How the headline was chosen

The headline is the BBC's framing, attribution included
("Zelensky says"), because a reaction story's headline must show
whose words it carries. The Kremlin's warning is in the context
record rather than the headline, which belongs to the principal
the wire pair named first.

## What was not printed

The Baltic reference in Moscow's warning is recorded in the
facts but not the headline: it belongs to a different theatre
and would have widened the headline past its cell.

## Watch next

Whether the Kremlin's escalation warning turns into a concrete
measure, in Libya or in the Baltic.
