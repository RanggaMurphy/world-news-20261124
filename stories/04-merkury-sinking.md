# Story 4 - The Merkury sinking (Around the World 1)

**Headline as printed:** Ukraine sinks Russian warship Merkury
off Libya, damages sanctioned Russian cargo ship

**Position:** Around the World, row 1, left cell. At fifteen
words it is the longest headline of the day, and the grid's
tightest fit: four lines at 19 pt.

**Carriers:** CBS News, Reuters (two, a wire pair with no shared
ownership: the cleanest confirmation in the grid).

**Region:** Libya / Mediterranean.

**Panel label defined:** UKRAINE (not drawn; the photograph was
usable).

## What happened

Ukraine's naval drones sank the Russian warship Merkury off the
Libyan coast, in a Libyan port, and damaged a sanctioned Russian
cargo ship in the same operation. The strike's news value is its
geography as much as its result: Ukraine's naval drone campaign
has mostly operated in the Black Sea, and a sinking in the
Mediterranean extends the war's map by a whole sea. Libya's
UN-backed government condemned the strike in Benghazi.

## The facts on record

- Ukrainian naval drones sank the Russian warship Merkury.
- The strike happened off the Libyan coast, in a Libyan port.
- A sanctioned Russian cargo ship was damaged in the same
  operation.
- Libya's UN-backed government condemned the strike in Benghazi.

## Why it is in the grid and not the features

It is a single military event, fully told by its headline. The
features are reserved for stories with a decision, an open toll
or a reversal inside them; the sinking is a fact, and facts at
this weight belong in the grid, where it leads the row.

## How the headline was chosen

The headline is the fullest of the carriers' versions because
the grid cell could hold it and because it carries three facts
(sinking, place, second ship) that the reaction story next to it
does not repeat. Shortening it would have saved space the page
did not need.

## What was not printed

Drone type and launch point: not in both carriers' copy. The
cargo ship's name: Reuters carried its sanctioned status, which
the headline prints; the name stayed in the linked reporting.

## Watch next

Russia's operational response in the Mediterranean, and any
Libyan move beyond condemnation.
